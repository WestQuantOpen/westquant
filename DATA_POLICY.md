# WestQuant Data Policy

## Data Ownership

Data generated from a user's algorithms belongs to the user.

When a user runs WestQuant's dataset generation tools on their own quantum
code, the resulting dataset is the user's property. WestQuant does not
claim ownership of, or training rights to, user-generated data.

## What This Means

- User-generated datasets belong to the user
- WestQuant does not automatically receive training rights
- Users may share data with WestQuant only via explicit opt-in
- "Share anonymized experiment data with WestQuant" is never default

## WQT Training Data

WestQuant's own training datasets (used to train WQT models) are
proprietary and unpublished. These datasets are not included in the
open-source release.

## Open-Weight Models

WQT10M-beta and WQT20 model weights are open under Apache-2.0.
The training data used to produce these weights is not published.

## Commercial Models

WQT3B and vertical models (WQT3B-Pharma, WQT3B-Chemistry, etc.) are
proprietary. Weights, training data, and inference infrastructure are
not published.
